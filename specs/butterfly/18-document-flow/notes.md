- Finding, not fixed here: the receivables and sales widgets of synapse-api show one currency (INR by default), and butterfly's sales and receivables summaries are in the document currency. About four in five orders are in dollars (all exports), so with INR as the default currency the sales, receivables and top-customer widgets leave out almost all export business, and the new dollar order above does not show in the rupee receivables. Exports, inventory and production are valued in rupees (company code currency) and are complete. The fix is a decision for the owner: expose company-currency amounts in the sales and receivables summaries (as SAP can) or convert at the fixed rate of 80 in synapse-api.
